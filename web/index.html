About This Site

Welcome to Plumbline's public status page. Plumbline is the public stability surface for the Filecoin Calibration testnet: a tFIL + USDFC faucet, the Calix chain-health console, and a long-running SP test target. Public commitments are documented in SLO.md; operational procedures in RUNBOOK.md.

Operated by TSE Reiersen (Org. 929 074 912). This is a calibration testnet surface, not a mainnet service. For urgent issues, DM @Reiers on Filecoin Slack.
